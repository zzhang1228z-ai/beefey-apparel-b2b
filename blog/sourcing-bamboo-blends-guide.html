The Buyer’s Guide to Bamboo Blends: Cotton, Linen, and Fleece

To build a year-round collection, you need more than just standard jersey. Mastering blends is the key to seasonal success.

1. Bamboo/Cotton: The Durable All-Rounder

Perfect for daily-wear tees and pajamas. Adding cotton increases the fabric's durability and structure while maintaining the signature bamboo softness.

2. Bamboo/Linen: The Summer Essential

Bamboo-linen blends offer the breathability and texture of linen with significantly less wrinkling and a much softer handfeel. It's the premium choice for luxury summer sleepwear.

3. Bamboo Fleece: For Premium Winter Comfort

Don't let the cold stop your bamboo sales. Bamboo fleece provides incredible warmth and a 'cloud-like' interior that outclasses traditional polyester-heavy fleeces in the premium market.
