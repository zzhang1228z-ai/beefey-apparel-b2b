Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Essential manufacturing and procurement details for global sleepwear brands.

Q: What is your Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ) for custom sleepwear?

A: Our MOQ is only 100 sets per design/color, which is highly flexible and perfect for startup pajama brands, boutique labels, and Amazon sellers looking to launch with low inventory risk.

Q: Do you provide free fabric swatches?

A: Yes! We offer a Free Fabric Swatch Pack containing our premium bamboo viscose, modal, and organic cotton fabrics in various GSM weights and popular colors. You only need to request it via our contact form.

Q: Can you handle custom private labels, hangtags, and packaging?

A: Absolutely. As a professional OEM/ODM manufacturer, we provide 100% custom branding solutions, including woven labels, heat-transfer tags, FSC-certified paper boxes, and bespoke packaging bags.

Q: What compliance certificates does your sleepwear factory hold?

A: Our factory is BSCI-certified and our fabrics hold OEKO-TEX Standard 100, GOTS, and GRS certifications, ensuring full market compliance in the US, Europe, and Australia.

Q: Do you support DDP shipping to the USA and Europe?

A: Yes, we provide full DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) shipping services. We handle all customs clearance, taxes, and final delivery, including Amazon FBA prep, ensuring a hassle-free import experience.
